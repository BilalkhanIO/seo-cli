1) Quick wins (do these first)

  • Write for one clear keyword (example: “prompt engineering for beginners 2026”). Use it in the title, first paragraph, and one H2.
  • Add 3–6 internal links to your related posts (examples below).
  • Add 2–4 authority outbound links (Google, Microsoft Learn, universities, official docs) to build trust.
  • Create an FAQ section (helps rank in “People also ask”).
  • Place ads smartly: one above the fold, one mid-content, one near the end (don’t overload).

2) GEO SEO (country targeting) that increases RPM/CPC

GEO SEO means writing and structuring content so it can rank in multiple countries. This is powerful because traffic from high-value countries (US, UK, Canada, Australia, parts of Europe) often pays more in ads and affiliates.

Use “country modifiers” in titles & headings

Examples you can copy:

  • “Best Free AI Courses (2026) for USA / UK / Canada Students”
  • “How to Earn Online as a Student in Pakistan (2026) + Remote Jobs for US/UK Clients”
  • “Scholarships in Turkey vs Germany vs Canada (2026): Requirements + Deadlines”

Create mini-sections for each target country

  • USA: mention “requirements”, “best tools”, “career paths”, “certificates”
  • UK: include “student discounts”, “government/official programs”, “internships”
  • Canada/Australia: “visa-friendly skills”, “remote work”, “study pathways”
  • Pakistan: “how to apply”, “documents”, “local examples”, “urdu keywords (optional)”

3) On-page SEO checklist (Blogger-friendly)

  1. Title: include primary keyword + year (2026) + benefit.
  2. First 100 words: repeat keyword naturally + who it’s for (students, beginners).
  3. Use headings correctly: H2 for sections, H3 for sub-sections.
  4. Short paragraphs: 2–4 lines max for readability.
  5. Add a “Contents” box: improves user experience and sitelinks.
  6. Image SEO: compress images, use descriptive file names, add alt text.
  7. One clear CTA: subscribe, download checklist, join WhatsApp/Telegram, etc.
  8. Update older posts: refresh titles to 2026, add internal links, add new FAQs.

Authority outbound links you can use (optional): Google Search documentation, Google AdSense Help, Microsoft Learn.

4) Internal linking strategy (fastest SEO boost)

Internal links help Google understand your site and keep users on your pages longer (better engagement = better ranking potential).

Internal link rules (simple)

  • Add 3–6 internal links per post.
  • Use descriptive anchor text (not “click here”).
  • Link from old posts to new posts too (this is huge).
  • Create 1–2 “pillar posts” and link supporting posts into them.

Example internal link block you can reuse:

Also read: Top Free AI Courses (2026) and try our Free QR Code Generator. For more guides, visit the homepage: Students Time PK.

5) Backlinks strategy (safe + effective)

Backlinks are still one of the strongest ranking signals. Focus on quality links from relevant sites, not spam.

Easy backlinks (beginner-friendly)

  • Resource pages: find “free courses resources” pages and request inclusion.
  • Guest posts: write 1 useful post for a niche blog (education, scholarships, tech tools).
  • Community backlinks: answer on Quora/Reddit (value-first) and link your guide when relevant.
  • Local directories: education resource listings (only legit ones).

High-quality backlinks (higher effort, best results)

  • Original data: publish a small survey (“Top AI tools students use in 2026”) and share results.
  • Free tools: tools attract natural links (like your QR generator).
  • Skyscraper updates: improve an outdated guide and reach out to sites linking to the old version.

Important: Avoid paid link farms, spam comments, and automated backlinks. They can reduce rankings.

6) Monetization that fits student-focused blogs

Ad revenue (AdSense / display ads)

  • Target GEO traffic with high-value topics (tech, software, finance, education tools).
  • Write “how-to” and “best tools” content (high intent).
  • Improve speed and mobile UX to increase ad viewability.

Affiliate revenue (recommended for Blogger)

  • Promote tools students actually use: hosting, VPN, online learning platforms, productivity apps.
  • Add an “Alternatives” section and a comparison table (even simple text tables work).
  • Include a short disclosure: “This post may contain affiliate links.”

Digital products (high profit)

  • Sell a “Scholarship document checklist (PDF)” or “Prompt pack for students”.
  • Offer a low-price product first (easy conversion).
  • Collect emails with a free download (“Free study planner”).

Sponsored posts (when traffic grows)

  • Create a “Work with us” page with stats (monthly visitors, countries, niches).
  • Price based on value: niche + GEO traffic + rankings.

FAQs

What is GEO SEO?

GEO SEO is optimizing content so it ranks for multiple countries by using country modifiers, localized sections, and content angles relevant to each target region.

How many backlinks do I need to rank?

There’s no fixed number. One strong, relevant backlink can beat many weak links. Focus on relevance, quality, and building content worth linking to.

What type of posts increase AdSense revenue the most?

Posts with high intent and valuable keywords: tools, comparisons, “best” lists, how-to guides, and topics that attract traffic from higher-paying countries.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Blogger (Blogspot) site rank on Google?

Yes. Blogger sites can rank well with solid on-page SEO — good titles, clear headings, internal links, schema — plus helpful, in-depth content and a sitemap submitted to Search Console.

How do I do SEO on Blogger?

Optimize each post's title and description, use one H1 and clear H2s, add internal links and schema, target search intent, and submit your sitemap to Google Search Console.

How do I get Blogger posts indexed faster?

Submit your sitemap in Search Console, add internal links from existing posts to new ones, and use URL Inspection to request indexing for important pages.

Does Blogger need a custom domain for SEO?

Not strictly — Blogspot subdomains can rank — but a custom domain looks more professional and is more portable if you move platforms later.

How do I optimize Blogger for AI search?

Answer questions directly, use clear structure with FAQs and tables, and add schema. The same clarity that helps Google also helps AI Overviews cite you.
