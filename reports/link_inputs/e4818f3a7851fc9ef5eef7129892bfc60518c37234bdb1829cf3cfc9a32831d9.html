Introduction: Why Etsy Sellers Need Dedicated Invoicing Apps

If you sell handmade goods on Etsy, run a boutique craft business, or handle custom commissions, keeping your financial records straight is essential. While Etsy handles transactions occurring directly on their marketplace, many creative entrepreneurs struggle with off-platform sales, custom wholesale orders, local craft fairs, and social media inquiries. If you send fewer than 50 invoices a month, you don't need a bloated, expensive corporate accounting suite. You need a streamlined, cost-effective invoicing app designed for micro-businesses.

This comprehensive guide explores the best invoicing software for low-volume Etsy sellers. We evaluate free tiers, payment gateway integrations, tax management, and workflow features to help you choose the right tool to keep your handmade business organized without cutting into your profit margins.


What Etsy Sellers Must Look for in an Invoicing App

Before committing to a platform, you must understand the specific requirements of handmade and low-volume retail businesses. Unlike large e-commerce enterprises, Etsy micro-sellers benefit most from specific core capabilities:

  • Low or Free Pricing Tiers: If you only issue a handful of invoices monthly, monthly subscription fees eat into your revenue. Prioritize platforms offering generous free tiers or low-cost basic plans.
  • Rapid Invoice Creation: Time spent doing admin work is time away from your creative workbench. Look for reusable item lists, customizable templates, and fast duplication tools.
  • Simplified Sales Tax & VAT Handling: Depending on where your buyers reside, you may need to collect and report sales tax. Your invoicing software should easily integrate tax lines.
  • Seamless Payment Integrations: Make it frictionless for your clients to pay you via credit card, Apple Pay, PayPal, or direct bank transfer links.
  • Professional Presentation: Aesthetic, branded PDF invoices build trust and reinforce your handmade brand's identity.
  • Reliable Record-Keeping: Easy export functions (like CSV or Excel downloads) simplify bookkeeping and tax preparation at the end of the year.

Top Invoicing Apps for Etsy Sellers (Low-Volume Options)

Here are the top-rated platforms that deliver stellar performance for low-volume sellers, balancing cost, usability, and professional output.


1. Wave – Best Overall Free Invoicing for Micro-Businesses

Wave remains a gold standard for freelancers and micro-businesses sending under 50 invoices per month. Its core accounting and invoicing tools are completely free, meaning you pay zero monthly subscriptions.

  • Cost: Free invoicing, accounting, and receipt scanning. Payment processing incurs standard pay-per-use transaction fees.
  • Strengths: Unlimited professional invoices, automated payment reminders, custom branding, and straightforward CSV exports.
  • Limitations: Customer support on the free tier is limited, and accepting credit cards requires using their integrated payment gateway.
  • Why it fits Etsy sellers: For off-platform custom orders and wholesale clients, Wave removes all subscription pressure, letting you keep 100% of your software budget in your pocket.

2. Zoho Invoice – Lightweight, Highly Customizable, and Free

Zoho Invoice is an intuitive, feature-packed app designed for independent creators and small teams who want deep control over their document branding.

  • Cost: Free tier available for small users with robust feature sets.
  • Strengths: Highly customizable templates, integrated time tracking (ideal for custom bespoke crafts billed hourly), client portals, and multi-currency support.
  • Limitations: Steeper learning curve for advanced automation features compared to more minimalistic apps.
  • Why it fits Etsy sellers: If you frequently deal with international buyers or recurring wholesale clients, Zoho's multi-currency handling and estimate-to-invoice conversion save hours of manual labor.

3. Invoice Ninja – Flexible, Open-Source, and Cost-Effective

Invoice Ninja caters to creators who want robust branding control and scalable invoicing workflows without hefty price tags.

  • Cost: Free self-hosted version available; cloud-hosted plans start at very low monthly rates.
  • Strengths: Clean interface, client portals, extensive payment integrations, and quote/proposal management.
  • Limitations: Cloud plans cap certain features, while the self-hosted version requires technical setup knowledge.
  • Why it fits Etsy sellers: It provides room to grow. If your handmade business eventually scales into a larger standalone e-commerce brand, Invoice Ninja scales right along with you.

4. PayPal Invoicing – Ultra-Simple and Instantly Familiar

Many Etsy creators already rely on PayPal for business transactions. Utilizing their native invoicing tool keeps everything under one digital roof.

  • Cost: Free to create and dispatch invoices; standard PayPal transaction fees apply upon payment.
  • Strengths: Instant brand recognition, high buyer trust, mobile-friendly interface, and zero learning curve.
  • Limitations: Basic visual customization and minimal reporting compared to dedicated accounting platforms.
  • Why it fits Etsy sellers: Perfect for occasional custom orders where you want a frictionless, no-subscription solution that your customers already know and trust.

5. Billdu – Best Mobile-First Invoice App for Craft Businesses

Billdu is built specifically for small service and product-based entrepreneurs who manage their businesses directly from their smartphones.

  • Cost: Low-cost tiered subscription plans designed for micro-volumes.
  • Strengths: Excellent mobile apps (iOS and Android), quick photo attachments for custom goods, and inventory tracking additions.
  • Limitations: Not entirely free, though entry-level pricing is accessible.
  • Why it fits Etsy sellers: If you travel to craft fairs, farmers markets, or pop-up shops, Billdu lets you invoice and accept orders on the go right from your phone.

Step-by-Step Checklist: Setting Up Your Off-Platform Invoicing

Follow these actionable steps to set up a professional invoicing workflow for your Etsy side hustle or primary craft business:

  1. Choose Your App: Select a platform from our list that aligns with your budget and technical comfort level (e.g., Wave for zero cost).
  2. Establish Your Branding: Upload your shop logo, set your brand color hex codes, and add your official business name and contact info.
  3. Draft a Standardized Template: Create a reusable template that includes clear payment terms, return policies for custom goods, and your Etsy shop URL.
  4. Set Up Payment Gateways: Connect your Stripe or PayPal account so clients can pay instantly via credit card or digital wallet.
  5. Organize Client Records: Keep a clean database of custom order clients, wholesale buyers, and recurring purchasers for fast future invoicing.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the best free invoicing app for low-volume Etsy sellers?

Wave is widely considered the best free option for low-volume sellers. It offers completely free invoicing, basic accounting, and automated payment reminders without any mandatory monthly subscription fees.

Do I need to send invoices for items sold directly on Etsy?

No. Etsy automatically generates receipts and order confirmations for items purchased directly through their marketplace platform. Invoices are primarily needed for off-platform custom orders, wholesale clients, and local craft fair sales.

Can I collect sales tax using these invoicing apps?

Yes. Most dedicated invoicing apps like Wave, Zoho Invoice, and Invoice Ninja allow you to add custom tax lines, sales tax rates, or VAT percentages directly onto your customer invoices.

How do I handle international clients when invoicing from my craft business?

Platforms like Zoho Invoice and Wave support multi-currency billing, allowing you to invoice international custom clients in their local currency while keeping your financial records accurate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best invoicing app for Etsy sellers?

The best option tracks your Etsy sales and fees, captures expenses like materials and shipping, and exports tax-ready reports — with pricing that suits low-volume sellers.

Do Etsy sellers need invoicing software?

Etsy handles checkout, but a bookkeeping/invoicing tool helps track fees and expenses, send custom invoices for off-Etsy orders, and prepare accurate taxes.

How do Etsy sellers handle taxes?

Report income and deduct business expenses (materials, shipping, Etsy fees) on Schedule C. Good expense tracking through the year makes this straightforward — keep records.

Can I send custom invoices as an Etsy seller?

Yes — for custom or off-platform orders, an invoicing app lets you send professional invoices and accept payment outside Etsy's standard checkout.

Is free invoicing software enough for Etsy?

For low-volume sellers, free tiers that track expenses and send basic invoices are often enough. Upgrade when your volume or bookkeeping needs grow.
