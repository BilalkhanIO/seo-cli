# Students Time Reach & Impressions Research

**Research date:** 2026-10-27  
**Property:** https://studenttimepk.blogspot.com/

## Executive finding

Students Time has an indexing and authority problem before it has a content-volume problem. Search Console shows the site is receiving impressions, but almost every query is ranking below page one. The best near-term opportunity is to strengthen the existing free-AI-courses pillar with current, verified course and certificate information rather than creating many similar posts.

## Current SERP research

Current results for free AI course searches are unusually focused on verification. Ranking pages repeatedly distinguish:

1. free access to lessons;
2. free certificates;
3. free digital badges;
4. paid certification exams;
5. courses that changed from free to paid.

Several competing pages were updated in October 2026 and explicitly rechecked provider pages. This creates a clear differentiation requirement: Students Time must show the date checked, provider, course cost, credential type, prerequisites, estimated time, and whether a separate exam or subscription is required.

## Required content improvement

Update the main pillar to a genuinely useful comparison page:

**Recommended title:** Free AI Courses With Certificates in 2026: 12 Verified Options  
**Primary intent:** free AI courses with certificates 2026  
**Secondary intents:** free AI course, free AI certificate, free AI badge, free generative AI course, AI courses for beginners

Required table columns:

| Course | Provider | Level | Time | Coding needed? | Free learning? | Free certificate/badge? | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Each row should link directly to the provider and use precise wording:

- “free course” when lessons are free;
- “free badge” when the provider issues a badge rather than a certificate;
- “paid exam” when training is free but certification costs money;
- “certificate availability varies” when the provider’s terms are unclear.

Add a visible **last checked** date and a change log. This is the strongest way to compete with pages that are winning on freshness and trust.

- Free AI Content Generation Courses in 2026
- AI Skills for Non-Technical Professionals
- AI Tools for Students
- Prompt Engineering for Beginners
- Machine Learning Courses Free in 2026 (after creating or improving it)

From each supporting article, link back to the pillar using descriptive anchors such as “verified free AI courses with certificates,” not repeated exact-match anchors on every page.

## Technical priorities

1. Resolve the homepage’s Search Console redirect error. The current public HTTP response is 200 and has a self-canonical, so run **Test Live URL** and request indexing again; Google’s stored crawl result may be stale.
2. Keep only clean canonical URLs in the sitemap. Blogger’s `?m=1` mobile URLs are expected alternate pages and should not be separately indexed.
3. Keep the 45 consolidated/off-topic posts as drafts unless there is a specific reason to restore one. The cleanup leaves a more focused AI and learning site.
4. Add one or two contextual links from already indexed pillar pages to every new article.
5. Add a clear author/editorial review line and update dates to support trust for course and certificate claims.

## Authority and distribution plan

The site has very little external authority, so publishing alone will not move positions quickly. Use a small number of relevant, human-reviewed distribution placements:

- DEV: adapted technical or learning article with canonical link to Students Time;
- Medium: adapted article with clear original-source attribution;
- LinkedIn: short summary linking to the pillar;
- relevant student/AI communities: answer a real question first, then link only when useful;
- one or two genuine collaborations or resource-page mentions.

Do not mass-publish identical backlink articles. Each external version should add a different explanation, example, or checklist and point to one canonical original.

## Measurement targets

Review weekly, but judge the plan over 4–8 weeks:

- homepage changes from Redirect error to indexed or successfully crawled;
- at least 10 clean URLs indexed from the sitemap;
- pillar impressions increase from 0–2 per query to consistent daily impressions;
- at least three queries enter positions 8–20;
- first organic clicks appear;
- external referral sessions from DEV, Medium, and LinkedIn are measurable in GA4.

## Recommended next action

Refresh the existing free-AI-courses pillar with the verified comparison table and October 2026 change log. Then add internal links from it to the two new Students Time articles and request indexing for the clean canonical URLs.
