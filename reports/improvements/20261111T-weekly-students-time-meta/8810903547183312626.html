What is in the six-course series?

Coursera currently lists six courses. The series moves from social media foundations and management to advertising, measurement, and a capstone:

  1. Introduction to Social Media Marketing — platform foundations, goals, and audiences.
  2. Social Media Management — brand voice, content calendars, and measuring post performance.
  3. Fundamentals of Social Media Advertising — ad creative and campaign planning.
  4. Advertising with Meta — campaign setup, audiences, budget, placements, and schedule in Meta Ads Manager.
  5. Measure and Optimize Social Media Marketing Campaigns — attribution, testing, dashboards, and return on investment.
  6. Meta Social Media Marketing Capstone — present campaign results and complete the program requirements.

How to check the cost before enrolling

  1. Open the official Coursera program page and confirm it is the six-course Meta program.
  2. Select the enrollment option and read the price, trial length, renewal date, and cancellation terms shown for your account and country before confirming payment. A free preview or trial is not proof that the complete program or shareable certificate is free.
  3. Keep the exam separate from the course cost: Coursera currently says the Meta Digital Marketing Associate exam and one retake are free for learners who complete the program. Later retakes may have a location-dependent fee.

Practical check: Before starting a trial, write down its end date and the amount that will renew. If the checkout does not clearly show the total and renewal terms, pause and ask Coursera support before subscribing.
